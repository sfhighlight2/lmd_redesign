Miracle Leaf

A 2+ year partnership built on local SEO for both clinic locations.

47%increase in foot traffic across both locations

The challenge

Running two clinic locations meant Miracle Leaf needed local SEO that worked independently for each address, without the two listings competing with each other in search.

Our approach

We built out dedicated local SEO for each location: separate Google Business Profiles, location-specific content, and a review strategy, then tracked both independently every month.

The result

“They generated a dramatic increase in foot traffic by optimizing SEO for both of our clinic locations.”— Carlo Suarez-Burgos, Miracle Leaf
